What you'll learn, skill by skill

IELTS is a strategic exam. We train exam technique alongside real English ability, for both Academic and General Training.

Listening

  • Strategies for every question type
  • Real exam audio simulations
  • Accent training: UK, US, Australia and more

Reading

  • Academic and General Training techniques
  • Skimming, scanning and time-saving methods
  • Spotting traps and distractors

Writing

  • Task 1 (charts, letters) and Task 2 (essay) breakdown
  • Grammar, structure and vocabulary training
  • Personalised feedback with band estimates and next steps

Speaking

  • Fluency, coherence and pronunciation coaching
  • Mock interviews with feedback
  • Cue cards and real question practice
