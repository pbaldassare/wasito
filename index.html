Academic or General Training

Both are run from the same WA test centre. Pick the version that matches what you need the result for.

Academic

For university & professional registration

Required by most higher education institutions and professional bodies.

  • Required for undergraduate and postgraduate study
  • Used by professional registration bodies
  • Same Listening and Speaking as General Training
General Training

For migration & work

Commonly required for skilled migration and work visa applications.

  • Used for Australian and other skilled migration visas
  • Focuses on everyday and workplace English
  • Accepted for secondary education entry

How to book your test

From choosing a date to walking into the test room, here is exactly what happens.

Choose a date

Pick a session and whether you need Academic or General Training.

Register online

Complete the registration form and submit valid photo identification.

Pay the test fee

Confirm your booking with secure online payment.

Sit the test

Arrive early on test day with your ID for check-in at the WA centre.

Frequently asked questions

What identification do I need on test day?

You must bring the same valid, original passport or identity document used at registration. Photocopies are not accepted.

Where is the WA test centre located?

Our primary centre is in the Perth CBD, with additional regional sessions scheduled periodically across Western Australia.

Can I choose between computer-delivered and paper-based?

Yes, availability depends on the session. Each date on the calendar shows which format is offered.

How soon will I get my results?

Computer-delivered results are typically available within 3 to 5 days. Paper-based results are usually available within 13 days.

Can I reschedule or cancel my test?

Rescheduling and cancellation are possible up to a set number of days before the test date, subject to the applicable fee policy.
